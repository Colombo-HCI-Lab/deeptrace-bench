#### AASIST

AASIST (Jung et al., ICASSP 2022) builds graphs over spectral and temporal nodes of a learned time-frequency map and combines them with heterogeneous graph attention, so one model covers artefacts that earlier work needed an ensemble for. The released weights saw only the ASVspoof 2019 LA training split, with no pretraining. A compact single-corpus baseline that sits near chance on In-the-Wild.

#### AASIST3

AASIST3 (Borodin et al., 2024), built for ASVspoof 5, feeds XLSR-53 features into AASIST and replaces some layers with KAN layers. The released weights saw ASVspoof 2019 LA, ASVspoof 5, MLAAD and M-AILABS on a front end pretrained on Common Voice, BABEL and Multilingual LibriSpeech, so the contamination guard refuses it on MLAAD-based evalsets. The repository says these are not the weights behind the paper's numbers, and the model card's Speech Arena re-scoring puts them far lower, so treat it as a weak multilingual baseline; parity is not checked yet.

#### AntiDeepfake MMS-300M

AntiDeepfake (Ge et al., NII Yamagishi Lab, 2025) inserts a post-training stage on real and fake speech between SSL pretraining and task fine-tuning. This model starts from MMS-300M (pretrained on about 1,400 languages) and is post-trained on 74k hours from 29 corpora in over 100 languages, including FLEURS, MLAAD, all of ASVspoof 2019 LA, LJSpeech and VoxCeleb2. The broadest multilingual exposure of any audio model here, so the contamination guard refuses it on MLAAD-based evalsets and anything built from FLEURS.

#### AltFreezing

AltFreezing (Wang et al., CVPR 2023) counters a 3D CNN's tendency to rely on either spatial or temporal cues by training the two weight groups in alternation. #### CORE

CORE (Ni et al., CVPRW 2022) penalises the distance between representations of differently augmented views of a face (crops, colour changes) to reduce overfitting; at test time it is a plain Xception frame classifier. DeepfakeBench's weights were trained on FaceForensics++ (c23 per the DeepfakeBench README) after ImageNet pretraining. It differs from the Xception baseline only in the training loss.

#### Effort

Effort (Yan et al., ICML 2025) splits each weight matrix by SVD, freezes the principal subspace and trains only the residual, which the authors argue stops fine-tuning from collapsing the feature space onto a few fake patterns. The paper targets AI-generated images broadly; here DeepfakeBench's Effort detector, trained on FaceForensics++ (c23) after CLIP pretraining on OpenAI's private data, scores face frames. Blocked since Google Drive refused the weight file as over quota on 2026-10-05; upstream also reports a Celeb-DF v2 reproduction gap and has no licence file.

#### F3Net

F3Net (Qian et al., ECCV 2020) splits each face crop into frequency bands with a discrete cosine transform and classifies the bands with Xception, since manipulation traces persist there after video compression. DeepfakeBench's weights were trained on FaceForensics++ (c23) after ImageNet pretraining. The detector's yaml names only NeuralTextures, which may not describe the checkpoint; the benchmark treats it as trained on all of FF++.

### Audio-visual (1)

#### HAVIC

HAVIC (CVPR 2026 Findings) pretrains its audio and video encoders on real videos only (LRS2) to model temporal and cross-modal coherence, then fuses them to score clips whose lips, voice or expression disagree. It was fine-tuned on a random 70% of FakeAVCeleb with an unpublished item list, so the contamination guard refuses it on FakeAVCeleb. The only open audio-visual detector here; it needs at least 3.2 s of video with a face in all 16 sampled frames (too long for HiDF), and parity is not checked yet.
