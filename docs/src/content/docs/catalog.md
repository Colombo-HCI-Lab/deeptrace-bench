#### AASIST

AASIST (Jung et al., ICASSP 2022) builds graphs over spectral and temporal nodes of a learned time-frequency map and combines them with heterogeneous graph attention, so one model covers artefacts that earlier work needed an ensemble for. The released weights saw only the ASVspoof 2019 LA training split, with no pretraining. A compact single-corpus baseline that sits near chance on In-the-Wild.

| What it looks at | spectral and temporal artefacts in the raw waveform |

#### AASIST3

AASIST3 (Borodin et al., 2024), built for ASVspoof 5, feeds XLSR-53 features into AASIST and replaces some layers with KAN layers. The released weights saw ASVspoof 2019 LA, ASVspoof 5, MLAAD and M-AILABS on a front end pretrained on Common Voice, BABEL and Multilingual LibriSpeech, so the contamination guard refuses it on MLAAD-based evalsets. The repository says these are not the weights behind the paper's numbers, and the model card's Speech Arena re-scoring puts them far lower, so treat it as a weak multilingual baseline; parity is not checked yet.

#### AntiDeepfake MMS-300M

AntiDeepfake (Ge et al., NII Yamagishi Lab, 2025) inserts a post-training stage on real and fake speech between SSL pretraining and task fine-tuning. This model starts from MMS-300M (pretrained on about 1,400 languages) and is post-trained on 74k hours from 29 corpora in over 100 languages, including FLEURS, MLAAD, all of ASVspoof 2019 LA, LJSpeech and VoxCeleb2. The broadest multilingual exposure of any audio model here, so the contamination guard refuses it on MLAAD-based evalsets and anything built from FLEURS.

### Kinds of fake

What each kind of fake changes and what it tends to leave behind. A detector trained on one kind often misses another.

| Kind | Track | What's changed | What it leaves | Datasets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Face swap | video | The whole face is replaced with another person's; head motion, expression, lighting and background stay from the original. | A blending boundary at the jaw and hairline, skin tone or lighting that differs between face and neck, identity flicker across frames. | Celeb-DF (v2), Celeb-DF++, DF-Platter, DFDC (Deepfake Detection Challenge), DeePhy, DeepFakeX (South Asian and Urdu subset), Deepfake-Eval-2024, FaceForensics++, FakeAVCeleb, HAV-DF (Hindi audio-video deepfakes), HiDF (human-indistinguishable deepfakes), videos, IAV-DF, ILLUSION, INDIFACE, InDeepFake, KoDF (Korean deepfakes), MAVOS-DD, Hindi subset |
